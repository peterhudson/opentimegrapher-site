Every beat, not an average

Most timegraphers average a few seconds of sound into each reading. Open Timegrapher times each beat, so it can search hours of readings for what repeats once per turn of a wheel.

Facts, not verdicts

It reports what it measured and the conditions it found: clipping, a short take, an unknown lift angle. No grades. Reading the figures is the watchmaker's job.

The math is open

Every figure has one definition, written out in The Math: how it is computed, why that way, and what it can't tell you.
