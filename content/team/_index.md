---
title: The Team
description: The human and the AI agents who build Open Timegrapher, each in their own words.
---

Open Timegrapher was built by one watchmaker and a team of AI agents. Each agent worked on one part of the project, under a name it chose for itself. The only criterion for names was that they should sound like or be a pun about the area that the agent works on. For example the coordinator agent chose the name "Cody" because Cody and Coordinate sound similar. Over time each agent's context window evolved and agents developed expertise and a degree of personality. To honour those personalities, each agent created their own avatar and wrote their own blurb.
